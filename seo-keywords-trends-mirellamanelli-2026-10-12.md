## 3. What this means

1. **Her #2 traffic page sits on a cooling trend.** Money piece demand is 25% below its 2022 peak and still slipping. Keep the page fresh, but don't build new content around it.
2. **The fastest-growing topics are ones she already ranks for, with weak support.** Her gray blending page gets about 971 visits/mo with **zero backlinks**, and the term has more than doubled in a year. Demi-permanent and color correction are both up 70-90%.
3. **The bleach page is her best asset and demand is rising (+42%).** But its URL and title still say "2025," it has only 3 referring domains, and two other pages compete with it.

## 4. Prioritized actions

1. **Refresh the bleach ranking page for 2026.** Update the title and H1 (keep the URL, or 301 it to an undated slug). 301-redirect `/the-best-bleach-for-dark-hair/` into it. Add an "hairdressing / salon bleach" section aimed at the "hairdressing bleach" keyword (#11).
2. **Build a gray blending cluster.** Expand `/best-hair-color-for-blending-gray-hair/` into a pillar post. Add supporting posts: "gray blending vs gray coverage," "demi-permanent hair color guide," and "gray blending highlights." Link every one back to the pillar.
3. **Push black-to-blonde from #8 into the top 3.** That's 6,600 searches and +50% demand. Rewrite with a stronger H1 for "black hair to blonde highlights" and link to it from the bleach page.
4. **Merge the color correction pages into one hub.** Demand is up 69%. Link it to the blonde-correction and black-to-blonde case studies.
5. **Fix the money piece cannibalization.** Take "Money Piece" out of the face-framing page's title so it targets "face framing highlights for dark hair" (1,300/mo, #4-5).
6. **Consolidate the foil placement posts.** When the new face-shape post goes live, 301 the two older placement posts into it.
7. **Seasonal timing:** refresh the pink highlights and hair tinsel posts by **May**, since both peak in late June and July. Bleach and color correction content peaks in **April to June**.
8. **Drop "foilayage" as a target term.** Lead the balayage-with-foils post with "how to do balayage" and "balayage techniques," which are its real traffic drivers.
