1. What Ubersuggest shows

Most of the lost keywords were long-tail rankings on pages 3 to 10 of Google (positions 51 to 100 fell from 12,514 to 461). Traffic fell far less, because the keywords that bring visits mostly held. September was the first real rebound in a year. Total keywords rose from 3,573 to 4,427, and page-1 rankings (positions 4 to 10) rose from 857 to 1,150, which lines up with the post rewrites since May.

Head to head, past year

On one shared chart, balayage gets about 10 times the searches of money piece hair. Among the mid-size topics: black to blonde hair 46, money piece hair 45, pink highlights 39, gray blending 36, best bleach for dark hair 7. Five years ago gray blending scored 7 on that same scale.

3. What this means

The #2 traffic page sits on a cooling trend.

Money piece searches are 25% below their 2022 peak and still slipping. Keep the post fresh, but put new content effort elsewhere.

The fastest-growing topics already rank, with little support.

The gray blending post gets about 971 visits a month with zero backlinks, and demand more than doubled this year. Demi-permanent color and color correction are up 70 to 90 percent.

The bleach post is the strongest asset, and demand is rising.

Searches are up 42%, but the title and URL still say 2025, it has only 3 referring domains, and two other posts compete with it.

4. Action plan, in priority order

  1. Refresh the bleach ranking post for 2026. Update the title and H1. Redirect /the-best-bleach-for-dark-hair/ into it with a 301. Add a section on salon and hairdressing bleach to go after “hairdressing bleach” (currently #11).
  2. Build a gray blending cluster. Expand the gray blending post into a main guide, then add supporting posts: gray blending vs. gray coverage, a demi-permanent color guide, and gray blending highlights. Link each one back to the main guide.
  3. Move black to blonde from #8 into the top 3. 6,600 searches a month and demand up 50%. Rewrite around “black hair to blonde highlights” and link to it from the bleach post.
  4. Combine the color correction posts into one hub. Demand is up 69%. Link the blonde correction and black to blonde case studies from it.
  5. Separate the money piece pages. Take “Money Piece” out of the face framing post's title so it targets “face framing highlights for dark hair” (1,300 a month, currently #4 to #5).
  6. Consolidate the foil placement posts. When the face-shape placement post goes live, redirect the two older placement posts into it.
  7. Plan around the seasons. Refresh pink highlights and hair tinsel by May, ahead of their late June and July peaks. Bleach and color correction content peaks April through June.
  8. Stop targeting “foilayage.” Lead the balayage-with-foils post with “how to do balayage” and “balayage techniques,” which are the searches actually sending it traffic.
