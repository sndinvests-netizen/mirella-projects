1. The tracking is set up wrong

The 0% is real, but it understates her chances, because the setup measures the wrong things.

What's off in the current setup
  • The brand is entered as “Hair education”, not “Mirella Manelli” or “Hair B&B University.” A mention of her by name might not be counted.
  • All 15 questions are local Mission Viejo searches about beauty schools and salon coaches. She no longer runs a local salon (Rebel Femme was sold), so she was never likely to appear.
  • None of the questions cover her strongest topics: bleach, money piece, foil placement, color correction, gray blending, or online stylist education.
  • Google AI Overviews isn't tracked, and that's where her top-ranking posts are most likely to get cited.

4. What would get her into AI answers

  1. Fix the tracking. Rename the brand to “Mirella Manelli” with “Hair B&B University” as an alternate name. Replace the local questions with her topics, for example: best bleach for dark hair, what is a money piece, how to place foils for face shape, online balayage course for stylists, best hair color education online, how to blend gray hair with highlights. Track behindthechair, jzstyles and uglyducklingcolor as competitors. This uses 1 of 100 monthly setup credits.
  2. Make her name easy for AI to recognize. Add Person and Organization schema, and build an About page that ties together Mirella Manelli, Hair B&B University, Manelli Hair and her YouTube channel. Use the same bio everywhere.
  3. Get mentioned on other sites. Guest education on behindthechair, podcast appearances, and brand-educator listings raise both Domain Authority and AI mentions.
  4. Write posts AI can quote. Open each post with a short, direct answer, then add the FAQ blocks from the May audit. Start with the bleach and gray blending posts, which already rank #2.
